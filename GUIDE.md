# 📘 Breakout — Strategy Guide

A complete guide to mastering **Breakout / Arkanoid Deluxe**. Read this to maximize your combos, master power-ups, and reach the high-score leaderboard.

---

## 1. The Goal

Clear every brick across all **5 levels** without losing all your lives. Dropping the last ball costs one life. Clear all levels to achieve arcade victory.

---

## 2. Controls Refresher

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Move paddle | `Mouse`, `Touch / Swipe`, or `←` / `→` (`A` / `D`) |
| Launch ball / Fire lasers / Release catch | `Space` or `Tap / Click canvas` |
| Pause / Resume | `P` |
| Toggle Audio Mute | `M` (or click top-right sound button) |
| In-game Guide | `H` |

---

## 3. Ball Physics & Aiming

The bounce angle is determined by where the ball contacts the paddle:

- **Center impact** → Shoots nearly straight up (safer, controlled).
- **Edge impact** → Deflects out at steep angles up to 60° (ideal for penetrating top channels).

**Pro Tip**: Slice the ball with the paddle edges to funnel it above the brick wall. Once above the wall, the ball will bounce between the ceiling and bricks, wiping out entire rows automatically!

---

## 4. Power-Up Breakdown

When a brick shatters, there is an **18% chance** it drops a power-up capsule.

| Power-up | Icon / Color | Duration | Tactical Advice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WIDE** | 🟦 `#4dd0ff` | ~12 s | Expands paddle width. Maximizes safety and catching radius. |
| **MULTI** | 🟨 `#ffe66d` | Instant | Splits active balls into multiple comet balls (up to 8 balls total). |
| **SLOW** | 🟩 `#38f9d7` | ~9 s | Reduces ball speed. Crucial when high-level balls get frantic. |
| **+1 LIFE** | 💖 `#ff4d8d` | Instant | Awards an extra life. Highest priority whenever you take damage. |
| **LASER** | 🔴 `#ff3355` | ~10 s | Mounts twin blasters. Press `Space` / `Tap` repeatedly to snipe bricks from safety! |
| **FIRE** | 🟧 `#ff7700` | ~8 s | Inferno Comet! Plows straight through 1-hit bricks without bouncing off them. |
| **CATCH** | 🟪 `#b366ff` | ~12 s | Magnetic Paddle. Catches the ball on contact. Line up your shot and press `Space` to launch! |

## 5. Combo Multiplier System

Consecutive brick hits **before the ball touches the paddle again** build a multiplier combo:
- 1st hit: `10 × Level × 1`
- 2nd hit: `10 × Level × 2`
- 3rd hit: `10 × Level × 3`
- ...and so on!

> 💡 **Bank Shot Bonus**: Sending the ball behind the brick wall creates 10x+ combos that send your score skyrocketing!

## 6. Level Progression & Difficulty

| Level | Hazards & Mechanics |
|---|---|
| **1** | Full 8×10 brick wall. Standard speed (`5.5`) and standard paddle width. |
| **2** | Ball speed increases (`5.95`). Gaps appear in the brick grid. |
| **3** | Ball speed increases (`6.40`). Top rows feature **2-hit cracked bricks**. |
| **4** | Ball speed increases (`6.85`). Narrower paddle and denser layout. |
| **5** | Maximum speed (`7.30`). Reinforced 3-hit bricks on top. Pure arcade reflex test! |

---

## 7. High-Score Strategy

1. **Prioritize the LASER**: When `LASER` drops, hold down `Space` or tap rapidly to clear tough upper bricks before the timer expires.
2. **Use FIRE for Speed Clears**: Fireball destroys entire columns in seconds without returning downward.
3. **Use CATCH to Snipe Gaps**: Magnetic catch allows you to stop the ball, position your paddle directly beneath a narrow channel, and launch the ball directly into the back row for massive combos.
4. **Don't Get Distracted by Power-ups**: If catching a power-up puts your ball at risk, let the power-up go. Survival is always #1.
